download the model at path/to/your/qwen-symbiont-contract-1/models/ https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct-GGUF?show_file_info=Qwen3VL-2B-Instruct-F16.gguf

rename it to qwen3-vl-2b-instruct.gguf

cd path/to/your/qwen-symbiont-contract-1

npm start

The terminal will boot up and pause. It will ask:

Please enter your full name to sign the symbiotic contract:

Type your name and hit Enter.

The console will light up, hashing the .gguf file, locking in your system prompt, and generating your symbiotic-nodule...json file.

It will then create the chat_log.txt.

When you see [NETWORK] Open your browser to: http://localhost:3000, open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) and type this into the URL bar:
http://localhost:3000/

You are now connected! Everything you type in the browser will be sent to the local JavaScript server, processed by the GGUF model, and logged with timestamps in your chat_log.txt.
